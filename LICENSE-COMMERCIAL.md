   white-label messengers, and bundled SDKs).
4. You sublicense, resell, or redistribute the Software, in whole or in part,

5. You operate a hosted service built on Vortex (e.g. a managed messenger,

   releasing the complete service source under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
6. You wish to remove the AGPL source-disclosure obligations from your product
   for any other reason.

You do **not** need a commercial license for:

- Personal projects.

  externally and is not made available over a network to outside users.

  (or a license compatible with the AGPL's source-disclosure obligations).
